Research

My current work focuses on representation, restoration, and compression for visual signals.

I am a member of the STRUCT group, which studies theoretical foundations and practical applications of image/video processing, multimedia communications, and computer vision. My current interests focus on low-level vision tasks where image quality, compact representation, and semantic understanding meet. Recent directions include image compression, video deraining, super-resolution, and generative coding with semantic guidance.
